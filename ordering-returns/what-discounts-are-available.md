### Saleae Discounts

We offer reduced pricing for students, educators, non-commercial enthusiasts, and early-stage hardware startups and independent contractors. To see if you qualify and to apply for a discount code, visit our [Discounts page](https://www.saleae.com/discounts).

### Quantity Discounts

Quantity discounts for all customers start at orders of 5 or more units. We offer the following:

* 5% off on orders of 5–9 units
* 10% off on orders of 10 or more units

If quantity discounts are needed, please [contact support](/contact) for more information.
