Saleae Logic MSO instruments can be calibrated when required for periodic equipment maintenance, internal quality systems, or compliance programs.

For calibration-file troubleshooting on Logic 4, Logic 8, Logic Pro 8, or Logic Pro 16, see [Device Calibration](/support/specifications-hardware/electrical-characteristics/device-calibration) instead. This article covers periodic calibration of the Logic MSO instrument.

## Unaccredited Calibration through Saleae

Saleae provides unaccredited calibration of the Logic MSO at no charge. [Contact Saleae Support](/contact) to request calibration and receive a prepaid shipping label.

When shipping to Saleae, only the Logic MSO pod is required. Probes, USB cables, the power adapter, and other accessories do not need to be sent.

## Accredited Calibration

For ISO/IEC 17025-accredited calibration, the Logic MSO may be calibrated by a qualified third-party calibration lab. Provide the lab with Saleae's Logic MSO performance verification guide before sending in the instrument:

<a href="https://downloads.saleae.com/specs/logic_mso_performance_verification_guide.pdf" class="content-ref">logic_mso_performance_verification_guide.pdf</a>

When shipping the Logic MSO to a third-party calibration lab, include:

* Logic MSO pod
* USB data cable
* USB power cable
* Power adapter

Analog probes and other accessories are not required unless requested by the calibration lab.

Transcat has successfully completed ISO/IEC 17025-accredited calibration for Logic MSO and may be an option for customers who need accredited calibration services.
